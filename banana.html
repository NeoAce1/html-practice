Fried Bananas Recipe

Fried bananas are a delicious treat that can be enjoyed as a snack or dessert. Here's a simple recipe to make them at home.

Ingredients:

  • 2 ripe bananas
  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 1/2 cup butter, melted
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  1. Peel the bananas and cut them into halves or slices.
  2. In a mixing bowl, combine the flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt.
  3. Add the melted butter and vanilla extract to the dry ingredients and mix until smooth.
  4. Dip each banana piece into the batter, ensuring it is fully coated.
  5. Heat oil in a frying pan over medium heat. Once hot, carefully place the coated bananas into the oil.
  6. Fry until golden brown on all sides, about 2-3 minutes per side.
  7. Remove the fried bananas from the oil and place them on a paper towel to drain excess oil.
  8. Serve warm, optionally with a sprinkle of powdered sugar or a scoop of ice cream.
